# Corrections after production

- **Status:** Draft


## Outcome

The owner can correct a receipt whose cost or weight was wrong after production used it, and can reverse a production batch, without breaking conservation or traceability.

## Boundary

- A lot's recorded unit cost stays immutable; corrections are new, auditable records.
- Stock, averages, batch costs, and trace stay consistent after a correction.
